Key Design Decisions

  • Warm off-white backgound (#F5F5F0) instead of cold gray
  • High contrast near-black text (#2D3436) for readability
  • Terracotta accent (#E17055) for progress bars, active states, interactive elements
  • Soft stone colors (#8a877e, #ece8e0) for muted/secondary elements
  • Clean cards with subtle borders and extra padding — breathing room
  • Serif headings (DM Serif Display) add editorial warmth
